# Third-party components

This compatibility file points to the release compliance record:

- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` — human-readable inventory and source links;
- `SBOM.spdx.json` — machine-readable SPDX 2.3 bill of materials;
- `licenses/` — license and notice texts;
- `compliance/SOURCE_OFFER.md` — written corresponding-source offer;
- `compliance/THIRD_PARTY_SOURCES.json` — exact source archive list;
- `compliance/LGPL_COMPLIANCE.md` — Qt/PySide6 replacement instructions.

Amirkhan Smagulov. No authorship or ownership of the independent third-party
projects is claimed.
